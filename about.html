about us

Who we are

Curator led emerging Art, Design, and Architecture. Formed in Los Angeles 2024. Forthcoming editions gather emerging and innovative artists, architects, and designers whose work engages embodied perception and integrates sensory input in their respective and cross-functional mediums of painting, sculpture, horticulture, architecture, time-based / new media technologies, artificial intelligences, automotive design, and more.

We believe that as artificial intelligence grows more deeply woven into the fabric of human life, one conviction remains central: the artistic process, and our experience of it, is irreducibly grounded in the sensory and embodied realities of being human.

How to reach us

We are actively looking for curators who are on the same wave length with us. Please drop us a line and contact us.
